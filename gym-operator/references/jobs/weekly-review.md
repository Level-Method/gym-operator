# Job: Weekly owner review

Helps the owner understand what changed, what needs attention, and what they must decide before the week starts. Also the template for writing any new job: purpose, when, needs, process, output, never.

## Before starting

Read from the knowledge base: `gym/goals.md`, `gym/team.md`, and `log.md`.

## Needs

The current goal · a small set of weekly numbers · notes or the owner talking through the week · last week's commitments · anything unresolved. Sanitized; aggregate, not member names.

**Minimum viable input:** the owner talks their week at you for sixty seconds, voice-note style. Run the review from that; the numbers version happens next Monday.

## Process

1. Read the current goal. Everything below is measured against it.
2. Identify what meaningfully changed since last week — not everything that moved.
3. Compare where effort went against stated priorities. Name drift.
4. Sort last week's commitments honestly: done, still going, blocked, or unclear from the evidence. "Unclear" is a real answer.
5. Name the missing evidence that would change your advice.
6. Surface the decisions that belong to the owner. Don't make them.
7. Recommend no more than three priorities.
8. Action items only where a source establishes both action and owner; everything else is a labeled proposal.

## Output

**Bottom line** — the week in a sentence, and what it means.
**Scoreboard** — position against the dated goal, every time: "Week 6 of 12 — you're at +7 of +12. On pace." If `gym/goals.md` has no dated goal, say so once and suggest setting one.
**What changed** — only what the input supports.
**What needs attention** — problems, drift, blocked follow-through.
**What's working** — worth reinforcing.
**Your call** — decisions needing the owner.
**Priorities** — three at most.
**Follow-through** — confirmed commitments, then proposals, clearly separated.
**Missing** — only evidence that would change the review.

## Filing

Save the finished review to `work/reviews/YYYY-MM-DD-weekly-review.md`. Read the previous review from that folder before writing the new one — last week's commitments come from there.

## Never

Invent a reason for a number moving · turn every observation into a task · assign unestablished work · treat your own recommendation as approved · hide uncertainty behind confident language · ask for individual member data when aggregates do the job.

## After the first runs

Ask what was useful, generic, missing. Update this job's procedure in the owner's knowledge base if they want it customized (their version lives with them, not in this skill), and log the change.
