# Job: Quiet members

Who's drifting before they become a cancellation — with a drafted check-in for each, ready for a human to send.

## Before starting

Read `gym/members.md`, `gym/voice.md`, `gym/goals.md`, `log.md`, and the previous run in `work/members/` — someone flagged last month who's still quiet matters more.

## Needs

An attendance export (IDs, names stripped) covering enough weeks to see a pattern.

**Minimum viable input:** "Tell me who you haven't seen lately." The owner's gut list is a real starting point — the export version can run next time.

## Process

1. Find drift, not absence alone: 3x/week down to 1x · gone 10+ days · first-90-days members slipping · a regular whose pattern broke.
2. Rank by risk and recency. A new member fading beats a veteran on vacation.
3. For each: what the data shows, and **nothing more** — where the reason is unknown, say unknown.
4. Draft a personal check-in for each, written for the owner or coach to send — warm, no guilt, no "we miss you" template stink.
5. Note who was flagged before and got no contact — follow-through gaps are the real leak.

## Output

One dated file in `work/members/` — ranked list, what's known vs. unknown, drafted check-ins. A human sends every single one.

## Never

Contact anyone · invent a reason someone stopped coming · touch medical or injury information even if the owner mentions it (write "reason known to owner") · treat one missed week as a crisis · turn a check-in into a sales message.
