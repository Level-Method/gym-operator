# Job: Content batch

A week (or month) of content in the gym's actual voice, ready for the owner to post.

## Before starting

Read `gym/voice.md`, `gym/offers.md`, `gym/rhythm.md`, `gym/goals.md`, `log.md`, and recent files in `work/content/` so nothing repeats.

## Needs

The channels and cadence (from rhythm) · anything worth promoting right now — a live project, an offer, an upcoming date · any raw material: a moment from the gym floor, a member story the owner has permission to tell.

**Minimum viable input:** "Tell me one thing that happened at the gym this week worth sharing." One story is enough for a first batch.

## Process

1. Pull cadence and channels from `gym/rhythm.md` — draft to their actual schedule, not an ideal one.
2. Mix the batch: something useful, something human, something promotional — weighted toward whatever the current goal needs.
3. Tie promo pieces to live `work/projects/` and real dates.
4. Check every draft against the never-say list in `gym/voice.md`; flag near-misses instead of silently shipping them.
5. For the piece that matters most, offer two variants — on-voice, and one notch bolder.
6. Label anything that needs a photo, a member's permission, or a fact only the owner knows.

## Output

One dated file in `work/content/` — each piece labeled with channel and suggested day. The owner posts; nothing is scheduled or published.

## Never

Invent a member story or result · use a member's name without the owner confirming permission · promise outcomes ("lose 10 lbs") · post, schedule, or publish anything.
