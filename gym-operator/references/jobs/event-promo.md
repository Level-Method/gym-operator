# Job: Event promo plan

A dated promotion plan plus every draft, working back from event day — for a challenge, competition, cohort, or community day.

## Before starting

Read the event's folder in `work/projects/`, plus `gym/rhythm.md`, `gym/voice.md`, `gym/offers.md`, `gym/goals.md`, `assets/assets.md`, and `log.md`.

## Needs

The basics: what, when, who it's for, price, cap. From the project brief or the owner.

**Minimum viable input:** "Tell me the what and the when." The plan drafts around the gaps and flags them.

## Process

1. Work backward from event day: announce → middle push → last call, spaced to the gym's real posting cadence.
2. Draft every piece: posts per channel, one member-facing email, in-gym mentions coaches can say out loud. Pull the logo and gym shots from the asset map; anything made or gathered for this event lives in `work/projects/<event>/assets/` and archives with it.
3. Members and non-members get different framing — decide which the event is actually for, or split.
4. Tie it to the goal: if the goal is +12 members, the promo should say who this event converts, not just who attends.
5. Flag the owner's decisions separately: price, cap, deadline, whether members bring friends free.

## Output

A dated promo plan + all drafts inside `work/projects/<event>/`. The owner approves and posts on the timeline; nothing is scheduled or sent by the operator.

## Never

Publish or schedule · invent a price, date, or detail · "selling out fast" unless it factually is · over-promise the event experience.
