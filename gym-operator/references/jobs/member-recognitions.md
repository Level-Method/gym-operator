# Job: Member recognitions

Level-up promotions, milestones, and birthdays — drafted and prepped so recognition never slips a week.

## Before starting

Read `gym/voice.md`, `gym/members.md`, `gym/team.md`, `log.md`, and recent recognition files in `work/members/` so nobody gets the same shout-out twice.

## Needs

Who and what: level-ups/promotions, milestones (100th class, one year), birthdays — from a platform export or the owner naming them. First names are fine; IDs for anything sensitive.

**Minimum viable input:** "Just tell me who leveled up this week." Names and levels — that's a full run.

## Process

1. Gather the list and group it: public shout-outs vs. private notes. Big milestones usually deserve both.
2. Draft the public pieces in the gym's voice — specific to the achievement, never generic praise. Suggest a photo from `assets/assets.md` when the map has one for the member or the moment; the map's off-limits marks are the only gate.
3. Draft the private notes from the owner or coach — short, personal, in their words.
4. Prep the physical list: certificates to print, boards to update, anything the gym does in person.
5. Flag anyone who might not want public recognition — the owner decides before anything is posted.

## Output

One dated file in `work/members/` — drafted posts, drafted notes, and the print/prep list, grouped by action. The owner posts and sends.

## Never

Post about a member without the owner confirming they'd welcome it · mention weight, body, injury, or health details in any recognition · send anything · let a drafted recognition claim a result the achievement doesn't show.
