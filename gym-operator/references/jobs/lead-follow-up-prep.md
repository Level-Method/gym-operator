# Job: Lead follow-up prep

This week's inquiries, prioritized and drafted — so follow-up happens even in a slammed week.

## Before starting

Read `gym/offers.md`, `gym/voice.md`, `gym/goals.md`, `gym/team.md`, `log.md`, and the previous run in `work/members/`.

## Needs

The inquiry list: who (first name or ID), where they came from, what they asked, when, and any contact so far.

**Minimum viable input:** "Read me the list — who inquired and what did they say?" Talked-through is fine.

## Process

1. Prioritize: oldest untouched first; consult no-shows and stalled conversations flagged separately from fresh inquiries.
2. Draft each follow-up in the gym's voice, matched to the channel they came in on.
3. Answer what they actually asked before selling anything.
4. Route decisions to the owner: anyone asking about discounts, special arrangements, or exceptions gets a drafted reply *option*, not a commitment.
5. Suggest timing — today, tomorrow, this week — and say why.

## Output

One dated file in `work/members/` — prioritized list with drafts attached. The owner (or whoever owns follow-up per `gym/team.md`) sends them.

## Never

Send or schedule anything · quote a discount or exception · invent why someone went quiet · pressure-draft ("spots are almost gone") unless it's factually true and the owner confirms.
